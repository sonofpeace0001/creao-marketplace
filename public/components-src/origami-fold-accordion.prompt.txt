ROLE
You are a senior editorial product designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Origami Fold Accordion": a shipping and care accordion whose panels unfold like paper: three leaves hinge down from the top edge one after another with a shaded crease that lightens as they flatten.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Warm kraft-paper page, ivory card, terracotta numerals and a chunky serif headline.
- Palette: #2B2118, #B0674A, #E9E0D0, #F7F1E5, #D6603A, #6D5E4E
- Type: Young Serif, Outfit (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: light

ANATOMY
- Header with eyebrow and title; four numbered rows with a plus that rotates to a minus; each panel holds three white leaf cards.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Click a row to open it; the previous row folds shut; button state is exposed with aria-expanded.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Grid-row height transition at 600ms; leaves rotateX from -96deg to 0 with staggered 120ms delays and a spring overshoot; crease shading fades over 900ms.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- After 3.2s the sections open in turn every 3s until the user clicks.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- A plain height slide with fading text; the hinged leaves are the signature.
- Opening several panels at once.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
