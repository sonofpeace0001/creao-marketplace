ROLE
You are a senior photography portfolio designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Lightbox Expand Gallery": a masonry grid of nine frames where a tile expands out of its slot into a centred lightbox with a caption, and flies back into place on close.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Warm cream gallery card with serif title, softly blurred scrim and a caption card.
- Palette: #1B1812, #E8590C, #EDE9E0, #FBF8F1, #9A917B, #8F866F
- Type: Instrument Serif, Inter Tight, Geist Mono (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: light

ANATOMY
- Header; three-column masonry of varied-height tiles with hover captions; blurred scrim; floating lightbox; caption card; close button.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Click a tile to open, click the scrim, close button or press Escape to return; tiles keep their place while hidden.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- The lightbox starts at the tile's exact rectangle and transitions left, top, width, height and radius over 600ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.1,.3,1); scrim fades 450ms; caption springs up with a .25s delay.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- Frames open and close in turn, each held for 2.8s.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- A fade-in modal with no spatial link to the tile.
- Layout shift in the grid when a tile is lifted out.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
