ROLE
You are a senior creative developer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Tilt Glare Button": call-to-action buttons that tilt toward the cursor in 3D while a glare and holographic sheen slide across their faces.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Ultraviolet night with a violet to pink to amber gradient and a graphite twin.
- Palette: #2A1A55, #0B0A18, #F1EEFF, #8E86B8, #7C3AED, #EC4899
- Type: Sora (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Headline and intro; a holographic gradient button and a dark glass button, each with an icon.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Pointer position sets perspective rotation up to 13 degrees plus the glare centre and sheen offset; the label floats at translateZ; eases back on leave.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Max tilt 13 degrees on Y and 11 on X under perspective 700px; label at translateZ(40px).
- Glare: 240px radial white at .55 with soft-light blend; sheen: 115 degree band at 46% with overlay blend, offset by pointer X.
- Leaving eases back over 500ms cubic-bezier(.2,1.2,.4,1).

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- Both buttons sway slowly out of phase and sweep their glare (rotateY sin(0.9t) x 14, rotateX cos(0.7t) x 9).
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- Tilt without a glare, which reads flat.
- Tilting the label away from the face; keep it floating above.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
