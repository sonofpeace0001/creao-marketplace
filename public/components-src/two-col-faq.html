Quick answers

Billing

Will I be charged when my trial ends?

Only if you add a card. Without one, your workspace simply moves to the free plan — nothing is billed.

Security

Is my data encrypted?

Always. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and per-workspace keys on the Enterprise plan.

Setup

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live in under 15 minutes. Import from CSV, Notion or Trello with a single upload.

Billing

Can I get an invoice for my accountant?

Every payment generates a PDF invoice with your company details, available under Billing → History.
