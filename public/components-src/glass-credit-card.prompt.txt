ROLE
You are a senior fintech product designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Glass Credit Card": a fintech payment card with a chip, a drifting light sweep and a full 3D flip to a back with signature strip and CVV.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Emerald glass with a gold chip, orbit rings and a single warm accent on near-black.
- Palette: #E9F3EE, #0A0F0D, #0F2A22, #FACC15, #F3D98B, #C79A33
- Type: Manrope, Martian Mono (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Card front: logo, chip, spaced number, cardholder, expiry, network marks; card back: stripe, signature and CVV, fine print; Flip and Freeze controls.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Flip rotates 180 degrees on the Y axis; Freeze desaturates the card and relabels the control.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Flip: 900ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.1,.3,1), backface-visibility hidden on both faces, resting rotateX 6 degrees.
- Sheen: a 105 degree highlight band translating -60% to 60% over 5s ease-in-out, on a loop.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- The card flips every 3.4s after a 3.6s head start; the sheen runs continuously.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- A card that flips without perspective, which looks like a scaleX trick.
- Card numbers in a proportional font.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
