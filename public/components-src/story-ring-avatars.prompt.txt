ROLE
You are a senior social product designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Story Ring Avatars": a stories row with gradient rings that spin for unseen stories, collapse to grey with a tick when viewed, and pop back when new stories arrive.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Near-black stage with an amber, pink and violet ring, grey seen state, and a viewer panel with segmented progress.
- Palette: #0C0D14, #F59E0B, #343748, #EEF0FA, #767A98, #EC4899
- Type: Outfit (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Row of seven story avatars plus an add-your-story tile; viewer panel with large avatar, name, meta and three progress segments.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Clicking a story plays its progress bars, then marks it seen: the ring turns grey and shrinks slightly and a green tick badge pops in.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Ring: conic gradient with a registered @property angle rotating over 3.2s; seen badge scales in over 400ms spring; new story ring pops from scale .3 over 700ms; progress segments fill in 10 steps of 45ms.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- Stories are viewed one after another; once all are seen, new ones arrive with a pop and the cycle repeats.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- A static gradient ring.
- Seen state that only changes colour; it also shrinks and ticks.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
