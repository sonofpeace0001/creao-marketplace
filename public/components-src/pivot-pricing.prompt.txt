ROLE
You are a senior product designer and conversion-focused front-end engineer (15+ years, fintech and B2B SaaS pricing pages). Build "Ledger Pricing": a three-tier pricing section for an accounting product, with a working billing toggle and a seat stepper.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that makes the middle plan the obvious choice without tricks, and lets visitors see real price changes.

ART DIRECTION
- Warm, trustworthy, editorial: cream #F6F1E7, paper #FFFDF8, forest #12372A, sage #436850, lime accent #C9E265, ink #1B1B18.
- Type: DM Serif Display for the headline, plan names and prices (price 60px, -0.03em); DM Sans for UI and copy.
- Shape: 22px card radius, 1px forest-tinted borders, the featured card inverted to solid forest and lifted 10px.

ANATOMY
1. Eyebrow "Pricing", headline "Pricing that grows with your books." with the last three words italic sage, one-sentence subhead.
2. Segmented billing toggle (Monthly / Annual) with a sliding forest thumb and a lime "2 months free" tag.
3. Three cards: Solo ($0), Studio ($29 → $24/mo annual, "Most chosen" lime ribbon), Firm ($79 base with a team-seat stepper). Each has a one-line audience, a checklist (check icons; one unavailable item struck through with a dash), and a CTA — outlined for Solo/Firm, lime-filled on Studio.
4. Fine print: currency, seat pricing, cancellation.

INTERACTION
- Toggling billing animates every price with a 380ms eased count, shows a struck-through monthly price, and changes the unit label to "/ month, billed yearly".
- Firm's stepper (min 5, max 50) adds $9 per extra seat monthly ($7 annual) and re-animates the price.
- Cards lift 4px on hover. Toggle buttons use aria-pressed; stepper buttons have aria-labels and the seat count is aria-live.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single file, inline CSS and ~20 lines of JS, Google Fonts via <link>. Inline SVG icons; no emoji.
- Responsive: single column under 920px with the featured lift removed. Honour prefers-reduced-motion.
- Real numbers and copy; no "Lorem" or "$XX".

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- Prices never show NaN or flicker through zero when toggling quickly.
- The featured plan keeps AA contrast for all text on forest.
- Annual savings are internally consistent across cards.
