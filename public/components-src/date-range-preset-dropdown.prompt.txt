ROLE
You are a senior analytics designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Date Range Preset Dropdown": a date range popover with presets on the left and a month calendar on the right where the range band floods across the days while a revenue figure and bar chart redraw.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Clean white dashboard, indigo range band with rounded caps, Geist type and tabular figures.
- Palette: #4F46E5, #6B7280, #EEF0F3, #9AA0AE, #F3F4F6, #0F1115
- Type: Geist, Geist Mono (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: light

ANATOMY
- Revenue header with range trigger (label and dates); revenue total, delta and bar chart; popover with five presets and a November 2026 calendar with range start and end caps.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Selecting a preset sets the range, updates the trigger label and span, and redraws the chart; clicking a calendar day sets a custom range ending today.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Range band: each day's highlight scales in from the left with a per-day delay proportional to its distance from the start; chart bars grow from 4 percent over 550ms with a 12ms stagger; popover enters on a 350ms spring.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- Presets are chosen in turn every 2.4s.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- Instantly colouring all the days; the band must travel.
- A chart that does not change with the range.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
