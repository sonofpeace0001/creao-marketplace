ROLE
You are a senior onboarding designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Swipe Card Onboarding": a topic-picking onboarding where you swipe gradient cards right to follow and left to skip, with progress segments and a summary.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Peach page with neo-brutalist outlined cards, chunky shadows and Bricolage headings.
- Palette: #2A1608, #FF5A1F, #8A6446, #B6FFCE, #FFC2C2, #FFE9DA
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque, DM Sans (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: light

ANATOMY
- Heading; segmented progress bar; three-deep card stack with icon, title and copy and FOLLOW or SKIP stamps; skip and follow buttons; completion summary.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Pointer drag tilts and moves the top card and fades in the stamp; releasing beyond 90px flings it away and records the choice; buttons and arrow keys do the same; summary lists followed topics.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
