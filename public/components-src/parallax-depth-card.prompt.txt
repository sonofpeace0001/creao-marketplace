ROLE
You are a senior illustrator and creative developer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Parallax Depth Card": a travel card made of six depth layers, mountains, mist and birds, that shift against each other as the pointer moves.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Dusk gradient from violet to apricot, layered purple ridges, drifting pink mist and an italic serif title.
- Palette: #1A1626, #241B3A
- Type: Newsreader, Figtree (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Card with sky, sun, three mountain layers, foreground trees, two mist bands, two birds; shade; badge, title, copy and three trip stats.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Pointer offset normalised to -1..1 drives each layer at its own depth; the card tilts up to 6 degrees; text counter-moves.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Layer depths (px): -4, -9, -14, -22, -34 on X, 0.6x on Y; card rotateY 6 and rotateX 5 degrees under perspective 900px.
- Mist drifts 8% over 11s and 15s alternate; birds cross in 14s linear.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- A slow camera drift (x: sin(0.7t), y: cos(0.5t)) moves every layer without a pointer.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- Layers that move the same distance, which flattens the scene.
- Stock photography; the scene is pure SVG.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
