ROLE
You are a senior creative developer and illustrator with 15+ years of experience. Build "Holo Foil Card": a collectible trading card in a gold frame whose rainbow foil and glare slide across the artwork as the card tilts in the light.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Deep teal night, gold bevel frame, prismatic foil over a moonlit seascape.
- Palette: #F3D58A, #FF6B8B, #10333F, #061018, #E6F6F5, #B8862F
- Type: Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Name and HP header; framed SVG artwork with foil and glare layers; ability text; three stat tiles; hint line.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Pointer position sets rotateX/Y and the foil and glare centre; releasing eases back.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Max tilt 15 degrees on Y and 12 on X under perspective 900px; tracking 80ms linear, release 500ms cubic-bezier(.2,1.2,.4,1).
- Foil: 115 degree repeating rainbow gradient, size 260%, offset by pointer, color-dodge at .6 opacity, masked to the artwork box.
- Glare: 260px radial white at .55 with soft-light blend over card and art.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- The card sways on a Lissajous path (x: sin(0.8t), y: cos(0.6t)) so the foil is always shifting.
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- Foil laid over the whole card including text; keep it on the art only.
- A flat rainbow gradient that does not move with the tilt.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
