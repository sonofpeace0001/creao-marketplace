ROLE
You are a senior fintech product designer and front-end engineer with 15+ years of experience. Build "Tip Split Form": a bill splitter with a sliding tip selector, a headcount stepper with growing avatars and a per-person total that counts to each new value.

GOAL
One self-contained HTML file that looks like part of a funded product's real interface — specific, considered and never a generic template.

ART DIRECTION
- Mood: Deep forest green with a vivid mint result card and IBM Plex Mono figures.
- Palette: #4ADE80, #0D1B16, #06281A, #E8F5EE, #12261E, #7FA395
- Type: Sora, IBM Plex Mono (loaded from Google Fonts via <link>)
- Theme: dark

ANATOMY
- Bill input with currency prefix; four-option tip pill with a sliding thumb; people stepper with avatar stack; result card with per-person total, tip, total and round-up, plus a bill versus tip bar.

INTERACTION & STATES
- Typing a bill, choosing a tip or changing the headcount recomputes everything; the total eases to its new value; the bar re-proportions.
- Every interactive element has a visible :focus-visible state and a sensible hover/active state.

MOTION SPEC (exact values, do not approximate)
- Tip thumb 350ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.3,.4,1); avatars pop in with a 300ms spring.
- Total eases 14% of the remaining gap per frame; bar widths 400ms.

IDLE AUTOPLAY
- Four scenarios cycle every 2s (15% and 2 people, 25% and 4, 10% and 6, 20% and 3).
- Autoplay stops permanently on the first real pointer, touch or key event and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

AVOID (these make it generic)
- Totals that jump; they must count.
- Floating point artefacts such as 73.8399999; always format to two decimals.

CONSTRAINTS
- Single HTML file with inline CSS (and only the small amount of vanilla JS the behaviour needs). No frameworks, no build step.
- Inline SVG icons only — no emoji, no stock imagery. Real, specific copy; no lorem ipsum.
- Responsive from 360px up; honour prefers-reduced-motion; keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Semantic HTML with proper roles/labels where the component is interactive.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
- No layout shift, overflow or clipped text at 1440px and 390px.
- Every state described above is reachable and looks intentional.
- It could not be mistaken for a different component or another product's default styling.
