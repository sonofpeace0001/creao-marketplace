Field notes · Nº 07

On the quiet art of the pause

Good writing breathes. A divider is not decoration — it is a held note, a half-second of white space the reader gets to keep.

Some separators announce a new idea. Others only ask you to look up from the page for a moment before the next paragraph begins.

The typewriter taught us that every character costs the same, so the cheapest ornament was always a repeated one.
